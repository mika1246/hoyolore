# Teyvat Lore Research Pack --- Core Lore

**Research snapshot:** 24 September 2026\
**Purpose:** structured source material for importing into a bilingual
EN/RU Teyvat lore encyclopedia.\
**Scope:** foundational cosmology, ancient world history, the Archon
order, the Cataclysm, major cross-region historical anchors, and the
terminology needed to connect them.

> **Important:** This is a research pack, not a replacement for the
> game's primary texts. Claims are separated by confidence. Where the
> exact chronology is unknown, it is intentionally left approximate or
> relative. Community-maintained references were used as an index to
> locate and reconcile scattered in-game sources; the primary-source
> names listed under each section should be preserved in the
> encyclopedia's Source records and checked against the game text during
> final ingestion.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 0. Evidence legend

Use these values when importing the material:

-   **CONFIRMED** --- explicitly stated by a primary in-game/official
    source, or supported by several mutually compatible primary sources.
-   **STRONGLY IMPLIED** --- the text strongly points to the conclusion,
    but does not state the identity/relationship directly.
-   **APPROXIMATE** --- broadly established, but the exact date,
    duration, location, or sequence is uncertain.
-   **DISPUTED / UNRESOLVED** --- current sources permit multiple
    readings or contain an unresolved contradiction.
-   **THEORY --- DO NOT IMPORT AS FACT** --- useful interpretive
    possibility only.

For historical dates, prefer: - `exact` - `approximate` - `range` -
`relative` - `unknown`

Do not manufacture a BP number for events known only by relative order.

# 1. World model: the minimum conceptual framework

## 1.1 Teyvat

**Type:** Term / World\
**RU:** Тейват\
**Status:** CONFIRMED

Teyvat is the world in which the main story takes place. Its present
order is not its original order. The setting preserves traces of several
successive world systems: the ancient dragon world, the order imposed by
the Heavenly Principles, later divine and human civilizations, the
Seven-Archon system, and incursions of powers described as coming from
outside the world.

For database purposes, avoid treating "Teyvat" as synonymous with the
current seven nations. Historical material repeatedly refers to earlier
civilizations, territories outside the Seven's present jurisdiction,
subterranean realms, the Dark Sea, the Abyss, and structures that
predate the current political order.

**Key relationships:** Seven Sovereigns; Heavenly Principles; Four
Shades; Descenders; Celestia; Abyss; Irminsul; Seven Nations.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1.2 Light Realm, Human Realm, Void Realm

**Type:** Cosmology terms\
**Status:** CONFIRMED at broad level; details should remain
source-specific.

A useful high-level model distinguishes powers native to different
"realms" or world systems.

-   **Light Realm:** associated with elemental beings and dragonkind.
-   **Human Realm:** the world order in which humanity and gods of the
    current system exist.
-   **Void Realm:** associated with the Abyss and powers fundamentally
    incompatible with Teyvat's ordinary world order.

Do not flatten every unusual power into one of these categories unless
the relevant source does so.

**Primary-source anchors:** Enkanomiya / Three Realms Gateway Offering
material; dragon and vishap lore; later Abyss and Sovereign material.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 2. The Old World: dragons before the present order

## 2.1 Seven Sovereigns

**Type:** Collective / historical rulers\
**RU:** Семь Владык / elemental Dragon Sovereigns --- preserve official
localization used by the project\
**Status:** CONFIRMED

Before the current heavenly order, Teyvat was ruled by ancient elemental
dragons. Seven great sovereigns corresponded to the elemental powers of
the world. The dragon order is repeatedly framed as the world's original
or pre-usurpation order.

The later divine order did not simply emerge naturally from this system:
the old rulers were defeated and elemental Authorities were taken from
them.

Known or especially important Sovereign-related entities include: -
**Nibelung** --- Dragon King, central to the ancient conflict. -
**Apep** --- Dendro dragon, survivor of ancient wars. - **Neuvillette**
--- the reborn Hydro Dragon Sovereign in the present era. - other
elemental Sovereigns should be represented individually only where the
game supplies enough evidence.

**Do not state:** that every named powerful dragon is automatically one
of the Seven Sovereigns.

**Related events:** Arrival of the Heavenly Principles; defeat of the
old dragon order; War of Funerary Flame; restoration of Hydro Authority
to Neuvillette.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2.2 Nibelung

**Type:** Historical entity / Dragon King\
**Status:** CONFIRMED core; Descender classification details remain
UNRESOLVED.

Nibelung is the ancient Dragon King and one of the most important
figures in the pre-human history of Teyvat.

Current lore establishes a broad sequence:

1.  Nibelung belonged to the original dragon order.
2.  At some point he departed / was absent from Teyvat.
3.  The Heavenly Principles arrived and established a new order.
4.  Nibelung later returned with power associated with the Abyss /
    forbidden knowledge.
5.  He waged a devastating war against the Heavenly Principles, now
    identified in current lore as the **War of Funerary Flame**.
6.  The conflict catastrophically damaged the world.
7.  The Heavenly Principles ultimately prevailed, but were severely
    weakened.

Recent lore complicates the term **Descender**: Nibelung can be
discussed in relation to Descender-like status, but the numbering and
exact criteria are not simple. Do not force him into a numbered slot
unless a primary source explicitly resolves it.

**Evidence caution:** older lore framed the ancient enemy through the
"Second Who Came" language of *Before Sun and Moon*. Newer material
supplies additional identities and context. Preserve the older source as
an in-universe historical account rather than silently replacing its
wording with a modern interpretation.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 3. The Heavenly Principles and the imposed world order

## 3.1 Heavenly Principles

**Type:** Cosmological / ruling entity or principle\
**RU:** Небесный Порядок / Небесные принципы --- use the official
localization appropriate to each source\
**Status:** CONFIRMED as a central ruling power; exact ontology remains
partly UNRESOLVED.

The Heavenly Principles are associated with the power that imposed a new
order on Teyvat, defeated the ancient dragon rulers, altered the world's
rules, and became the foundation of the later celestial system.

The term must not automatically be treated as perfectly interchangeable
with every related expression: - Primordial One - First Descender -
Celestia - Sustainer of Heavenly Principles - "the heavens" - "the
throne of the heavens"

Some sources strongly connect these ideas, but they can refer to
different persons, institutions, manifestations, or perspectives.

Create separate entities and connect them with evidence-bearing claims
rather than collapsing them.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 3.2 Primordial One / Phanes

**Type:** Historical/cosmological entity\
**Status:** CONFIRMED that *Before Sun and Moon* describes a Primordial
One; identity as "Phanes" is presented by that text with uncertainty.

The Enkanomiyan book *Before Sun and Moon* preserves a foundational
account:

-   an "eternal throne of the heavens" arrived;
-   the Primordial One fought the seven dragon sovereigns;
-   the Primordial One created four shining shades;
-   the struggle against the Sovereigns lasted forty years in the book's
    chronology;
-   after victory, the world was remade for humanity;
-   the text says the Primordial One **may have been Phanes**, rather
    than presenting the identification as an unquestionable fact.

This distinction is important. Store:

`Primordial One` as the entity.

Store `Phanes` as an alias/possible identity with an evidence note, not
necessarily as an unqualified canonical personal name.

**Primary source:** *Before Sun and Moon*.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 3.3 Four Shades

**Type:** Collective / cosmological entities\
**Status:** CONFIRMED

The Primordial One is described as having four "shades." Later lore
associates major world Authorities with this framework.

Important known or strongly established figures include: - **Istaroth**
--- associated with Time. - **Ronova** --- associated with Death. - the
**Ruler of Life** (associated in Fontaine material with the creation of
Egeria; name/identity should follow the latest primary text). - the
remaining Shade/Authority should be entered according to current
explicit source material, not filled from theory.

Recent lunar lore adds important prehistory to the Authorities now
associated with the Shades. Treat the chronology carefully:
powers/Authorities can predate their current holders.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 4. Three Moons and ancient lunar order

## 4.1 Three Moons / Moon Goddesses

**Type:** Collective / ancient entities\
**Status:** CONFIRMED in current lore; many details are recent and
should retain patch/version metadata.

Current lore substantially expands what older sources presented only as
legends of three moon sisters.

The Three Moons were connected to the ancient dragon world and possessed
major Authorities. Newer material identifies the lunar system as
predating the current heavenly order and links the sisters to powers
corresponding to fundamental world laws.

Current research index identifies: - **Aria** - **Sonnet** - **Canon**

During the later ancient war, their moons became entangled in the
conflict. Two moons were destroyed and the Frost Moon was cast beyond
the false sky. The surviving/remaining lunar legacy becomes important
again in the Nod-Krai / Song of the Welkin Moon era.

Because this lore is patch-sensitive and recent, every Moon-related
claim should carry `introducedInVersion` and primary source references.

**Do not merge:** old poetic myths about the moon sisters with modern
revealed history without indicating which details come from which
source.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 5. Creation/remaking of the human world

## 5.1 Defeat of the Sovereigns

**Type:** HistoricalEvent\
**Date:** ancient; relative chronology.\
**Status:** CONFIRMED.

According to *Before Sun and Moon*, the Primordial One and its four
shades fought the Seven Sovereigns. The account gives a forty-year
duration before the Sovereigns were defeated and the new ruler began
reshaping the world.

The "forty years" belongs to the internal chronology of this ancient
text. Do not convert it into a BP date.

**Regions:** world-scale / Teyvat\
**Participants:** Primordial One; Four Shades; Seven Sovereigns\
**Sources:** *Before Sun and Moon*; later dragon/Sovereign lore for
reinterpretation.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 5.2 Creation/remaking for humanity

**Type:** HistoricalEvent / Era transition\
**Status:** CONFIRMED according to the Enkanomiyan account.

*Before Sun and Moon* describes the world being reshaped after the
dragons' defeat. Mountains, rivers, seas, plants, animals, and
eventually humanity appear in the narrative of the new order. Humanity
then enters a covenant-like relationship with the Primordial One.

Treat this as an **in-universe historical account**. The book is
enormously important but is still a document written from a particular
ancient human tradition.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 6. The War of Funerary Flame

## 6.1 Core event

**Type:** HistoricalEvent\
**Date:** ancient; roughly associated by several regional histories with
events around \~6000 BP, but the war itself should not be assigned a
single exact BP year unless a source explicitly does so.\
**Status:** CONFIRMED; internal sequence partly APPROXIMATE.

The War of Funerary Flame is the great ancient conflict between
Nibelung's returning dragon order and the Heavenly Principles.

Broadly supported sequence:

1.  Nibelung returned to Teyvat after obtaining/bringing power from
    beyond the ordinary world order, associated with the Abyss and
    forbidden knowledge.
2.  He launched a war of vengeance against the Heavenly Principles.
3.  The conflict devastated heaven and earth.
4.  Abyssal contamination threatened the stability of the world.
5.  Celestial Nails were cast down as part of attempts to
    stabilize/purify damaged regions.
6.  The Heavenly Principles ultimately defeated Nibelung.
7.  The Heavenly Principles were themselves gravely weakened by the
    conflict.
8.  The aftermath contributed to the later establishment of a new divine
    order.

**Critical:** this event has major simultaneous regional consequences
and should be a top-level timeline node with linked child events rather
than one giant prose entry.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 6.2 Enkanomiya falls

**Type:** HistoricalEvent\
**Date:** during War of Funerary Flame\
**Status:** CONFIRMED.

During the ancient catastrophe, the land that became Enkanomiya fell
into the depths and was cut off from the surface.

Its people called to the ruling heavenly powers. *Before Sun and Moon*
says that Istaroth did not abandon them, while the others did not
answer.

This event should link: - Enkanomiya - Istaroth - War of Funerary
Flame - Dragonheirs/Vishaps - *Before Sun and Moon*

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 6.3 Sumeru / Great Red Sand transformation

**Type:** HistoricalEvent\
**Date:** ancient, around the same broad catastrophic period; often
indexed around \~6000 BP.\
**Status:** CONFIRMED broad event; exact dating APPROXIMATE.

A Celestial Nail and the ancient war are connected to catastrophic
environmental transformation in Sumeru. Apep's history and later Sumeru
sources frame this period as a major rupture between the old dragon
world and the new order.

Do not conflate this ancient catastrophe with the much later
forbidden-knowledge disaster caused by King Deshret. They are separate
events.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 6.4 Natlan's Ley Lines are devastated

**Type:** HistoricalEvent\
**Date:** War of Funerary Flame\
**Status:** CONFIRMED.

The ancient war severely damaged Natlan's Ley Lines. The later **Night
Kingdom** functions as a reconstructed/substitute network associated
with the Lord of the Night and guidance from Ronova.

This is a crucial example of why the map and timeline need historical
state: - ancient Natlan before damage; - post-war damaged Ley Line
state; - reconstructed Night Kingdom; - later strengthening under
human/Archon systems.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 6.5 Ancient lunar catastrophe

**Type:** HistoricalEvent\
**Date:** War of Funerary Flame\
**Status:** CONFIRMED core; details recent.

The lunar order was violently transformed during the war. Current lore
links Nibelung, the Moon Goddesses, the weaponization/destruction of
moons, and the later false-sky lunar state.

Because this material is recent, preserve the exact primary sources and
avoid back-projecting later explanations into old books without
annotation.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 7. Celestial Nails

**Type:** Term / class of objects\
**Status:** CONFIRMED

Celestial Nails are heavenly objects associated with major ancient
catastrophes and with the suppression/purification/stabilization of
corrupted regions.

Known sites and related phenomena include: - Dragonspine / Sal
Vindagnyr - The Chasm / Spiritstone - Sumeru desert-related ancient
devastation - Natlan / Night Kingdom context - other sites as supported
by current primary lore

A Nail's presence should not automatically be interpreted as "Celestia
destroyed a civilization because it learned too much." That is an older
community simplification. Current lore gives a stronger role to world
stabilization and Abyssal contamination, while individual cases still
require their own evidence.

Create each Nail-related incident as a separate HistoricalEvent.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 8. Descenders

## 8.1 Definition

**Type:** Term\
**RU:** Сошедший / Сошедшая (verify official localization per context)\
**Status:** CONFIRMED concept; criteria partly UNRESOLVED.

A Descender is not simply "anyone from outside Teyvat."

Research within the story describes Descenders in terms of extraordinary
will capable of rivaling an entire world. This distinction explains why
an outlander may exist without qualifying as a numbered Descender.

The Traveler is identified as the **Fourth Descender**.

The Traveler's sibling, despite being an outlander in the ordinary
sense, is recorded by Irminsul as belonging to Teyvat and therefore
creates one of the story's central mysteries.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 8.2 Third Descender / Saarelainen

**Type:** Historical entity\
**Status:** Third Descender = CONFIRMED; identification/name Saarelainen
is strongly supported by newer material and should carry version
metadata.

Fontaine's Archon Quest established that the seven Gnoses were made from
the remains of the Third Descender.

Newer northern/Nod-Krai material names **Saarelainen**, the "Stranger of
Winter's Day," and strongly connects him with the Third Descender.

Important sequence: - Saarelainen lived after the War of Funerary Flame
and before the Archon War. - He became involved with Pohjola. - He was
killed by the Heavenly Principles after breaking a prohibition. - His
remains were divided and used in the creation of the seven Gnoses. - The
creation of the Gnoses also involved another figure described as "one
who came after."

Because the direct identity chain was revealed across sources released
years apart, preserve both: 1. the original Fontaine revelation: "Third
Descender's remains"; 2. the later Saarelainen identification.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 9. Gnoses, Authorities, and Divine Thrones

## 9.1 Gnoses

**Type:** Term / object class\
**Status:** CONFIRMED core.

There are seven Gnoses associated with the Seven.

Fontaine-era lore establishes that the Gnoses were made from the remains
of the Third Descender. Later lore ties this to Saarelainen.

Gnoses have strong elemental compatibility and can enhance elemental
abilities, but a Gnosis is **not identical** to an elemental Sovereign's
stolen Authority or to an Archon's Divine Throne.

This distinction is essential.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 9.2 Elemental Authorities

**Type:** Term\
**Status:** CONFIRMED.

The elemental Authorities originate in the ancient dragon order. The
Heavenly Principles took power from the Sovereigns and incorporated it
into the later world system.

Fontaine provides the clearest demonstration: - the Hydro Sovereign's
Authority had been incorporated into the Hydro Archon's divine system; -
Focalors destroyed the Hydro Archon's Divine Throne; - full Hydro
Authority returned to Neuvillette; - this is distinct from possession of
the Hydro Gnosis, which Neuvillette later gave away.

Therefore database relationships must keep separate: - Sovereign -
elemental Authority - Archon - Divine Throne - Gnosis

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 10. The Archon War

## 10.1 General event

**Type:** Era / distributed historical conflict\
**Date:** began several millennia ago; ended \~2000 BP.\
**Status:** CONFIRMED broad chronology; causes/details partly
UNRESOLVED.

"Archon War" is a convenient umbrella term for many regional conflicts
involving gods and the emergence of the Seven.

It was not one synchronized battlefield or one simple tournament.

Different regions resolved their conflicts differently and at different
times.

The final divine seat was claimed approximately 2000 years before the
present, establishing the Seven as the dominant divine-political order.

Current lore links the Seven-seat system to the post--War of Funerary
Flame restructuring of the world, but exact causal steps should be
represented as claims with sources rather than simplified into one
sentence.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 10.2 The Seven

**Type:** Organization / divine system\
**Status:** CONFIRMED.

The Seven are the Archons associated with the seven major nations and
seven elements.

At the end of the Archon War, the Seven-seat order was established.

Only Barbatos and Morax remained from the original Seven into the modern
era; other seats changed holders over time.

Important: "Archon," "god," "Gnosis holder," and "Sovereign" are not
interchangeable categories.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 11. Irminsul and Ley Lines

## 11.1 Irminsul

**Type:** Term / world system\
**Status:** CONFIRMED.

Irminsul is deeply connected to Teyvat's information, memory, and
world-record structure.

Key properties demonstrated by Sumeru: - information stored in Irminsul
affects the memories and records of beings belonging to Teyvat; -
removing a person/information from Irminsul can rewrite how history is
remembered and documented; - the physical events themselves are not
simply "undone"; the world's accessible record and causal explanations
reorganize around the missing information; - Descender-related
exceptions are crucial to understanding the limits of this system.

The Greater Lord Rukkhadevata crisis demonstrates that corruption within
Irminsul can affect Teyvat on a vast scale.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 11.2 Ley Lines

**Type:** Term / world system\
**Status:** CONFIRMED.

Ley Lines are a world-spanning network closely associated with elemental
energy, memories, and the world's informational/ecological systems.

They can retain memories and historical impressions. Damage to them can
have enormous regional consequences.

Natlan is especially important because its ancient Ley Lines were
catastrophically damaged, requiring a substitute/reconstructed system in
the form of the Night Kingdom.

Do not write "Ley Lines = Irminsul roots" as a simplistic identity
unless the source used for a specific claim supports that wording. Model
them as strongly related systems.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 12. Forbidden Knowledge

**Type:** Term\
**RU:** Запретное знание\
**Status:** CONFIRMED.

Forbidden Knowledge is knowledge/power fundamentally incompatible with
Teyvat's ordinary world order.

Greater Lord Rukkhadevata describes it as knowledge that does not belong
to this world, originating from the depths of the Abyss and rejected by
the world itself.

Its effects include: - corruption of minds and bodies; - environmental
devastation; - corruption of Irminsul; - large-scale systemic
instability.

Major known outbreaks/uses:

### A. Ancient / Nibelung

Nibelung returned with Abyss-associated power/forbidden knowledge during
the War of Funerary Flame.

### B. King Deshret

Thousands of years later, King Deshret's pursuit of forbidden knowledge
caused disaster in the desert. Greater Lord Rukkhadevata helped resist
it, and Deshret ultimately sacrificed himself.

### C. Cataclysm

A second major forbidden-knowledge pollution struck Irminsul during the
Cataclysm 500 years ago.

Keep these as separate HistoricalEvents connected by the same Term.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 13. King Deshret's forbidden-knowledge disaster

**Type:** HistoricalEvent\
**Date:** several thousand years ago; after ancient world restructuring
and before Cataclysm.\
**Status:** CONFIRMED; exact date APPROXIMATE.

King Deshret pursued knowledge beyond the ordinary world order. The
result was a catastrophic outbreak of forbidden knowledge in the desert.

Consequences included: - collapse/devastation of his civilization; -
madness and disease among his people; - phenomena associated with the
Withering and Eleazar; - corruption that threatened the wider world.

Greater Lord Rukkhadevata intervened and nearly exhausted her power
helping contain the disaster.

Deshret ultimately sacrificed himself, ending that outbreak.

Apep later became affected through her connection to Deshret and the
forbidden knowledge he had obtained.

**Primary-source anchors:** - Sumeru Archon Quest, Chapter III Act IV -
Sumeru Archon Quest, Chapter III Act V: *Where the Boat of Consciousness
Lies* - *The Lay of Al-Ahmar* - Staff of the Scarlet Sands - Nahida
Story Quest Act II / Apep material

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 14. Abyss

**Type:** Term / power / realm-related concept\
**Status:** CONFIRMED broad concept; exact ontology intentionally
unresolved.

The Abyss is an otherworldly/void-associated power fundamentally hostile
or incompatible with Teyvat's ordinary order.

Do not conflate: - Abyss as a power/domain; - Abyss Order as an
organization; - Abyssal monsters; - forbidden knowledge; - Khaenri'ah.

They overlap historically but are not synonyms.

The Abyss predates the modern Abyss Order by an enormous span of time.

Nibelung's ancient war already involved Abyss-associated power thousands
of years before Khaenri'ah's fall.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 15. Khaenri'ah

**Type:** Civilization / Region\
**RU:** Каэнри'ах\
**Status:** CONFIRMED.

Khaenri'ah was a human civilization notable for existing outside the
ordinary rule of the gods and Seven.

It developed advanced technology and maintained a complicated
relationship with powers outside the ordinary world order.

Important concepts: - Eclipse Dynasty - King Irmin - Five Sinners -
Khemia - Ruin machines - Dainsleif - Pierro - Rhinedottir - Traveler's
sibling - Abyssal research - Cataclysm

Do not summarize Khaenri'ah as simply "evil civilization attacked by
Celestia." Current lore presents multiple factions, motives, failures,
and unresolved causal questions.

Likewise, do not state that every Khaenri'ahn supported Abyssal
experimentation.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 16. Five Sinners of Khaenri'ah

**Type:** Group / historical figures\
**Status:** CONFIRMED.

Five extraordinary Khaenri'ahn figures obtained/transcended through
immense power associated with the Abyss.

Known names: - **Hroptatyr** - **Vedrfolnir** - **Rhinedottir
("Gold")** - **Surtalogi** - **Rerir**

They are central to the events surrounding Khaenri'ah and later
Abyss-related lore.

Do not automatically attribute every action of the Five to the
Khaenri'ahn state as a whole.

Create individual character/historical-entity records.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 17. Rhinedottir / Gold

**Type:** Character / historical figure\
**Status:** CONFIRMED.

Rhinedottir is a master of Khemia, one of the Five Sinners, and creator
of multiple artificial lifeforms.

Her creations and research are deeply entangled with the Cataclysm.

Relevant entities include: - Durin - Albedo - Elynas - other creations
as supported by their own source records

Avoid reducing her to "the person who caused the Cataclysm." The exact
causal chain of Khaenri'ah's fall, Abyssal eruption, Celestia's
intervention, and the Sinners' actions remains more complex.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 18. The Cataclysm --- 500 years ago

## 18.1 Core event

**Type:** HistoricalEvent\
**Date:** approximately 500 BP\
**Status:** CONFIRMED.

The Cataclysm is one of the most important synchronized events in the
entire timeline.

Around 500 years before the present: - Khaenri'ah fell; - Abyssal
monsters and corruption spread across Teyvat; - multiple nations
experienced simultaneous disasters; - several major gods/Archons died or
disappeared; - Irminsul was polluted by forbidden knowledge; - the
Traveler's sibling witnessed Khaenri'ah's destruction; - the
consequences produced political and religious transformations still
active in the present.

The exact order of many Cataclysm events remains unresolved.

Model the Cataclysm as a **parent event/era cluster** with regional
child events.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 19. Cataclysm --- Sumeru lane

**Date:** \~500 BP\
**Status:** CONFIRMED.

During the Cataclysm: - forbidden knowledge again polluted Irminsul; -
Greater Lord Rukkhadevata fought the contamination; - because she
herself became contaminated, simply removing the external pollution was
insufficient; - she created/used a successor incarnation, Nahida /
Lesser Lord Kusanali, as part of the eventual solution; - in the
present-day Sumeru Archon Quest, Rukkhadevata's remaining polluted
existence was erased from Irminsul; - after the rewrite, inhabitants of
Teyvat remember the historical role differently.

**Critical database note:** This is the best example of why the
encyclopedia needs: 1. objective event records; 2. in-universe
remembered versions; 3. source/version notes.

Do not overwrite the "pre-Irminsul rewrite" history with only the
current in-universe memory.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 20. Cataclysm --- Inazuma lane

**Date:** \~500 BP\
**Status:** CONFIRMED.

Major linked events: - Makoto went to Khaenri'ah while Ei remained
behind initially. - Ei later reached Makoto and found her dying. -
Makoto's death led to Ei becoming the sole Electro Archon. - the Sacred
Sakura's unusual temporal history is tied to Makoto, Ei, and Istaroth. -
Abyssal forces attacked Inazuma. - Kitsune Saiguu was lost during the
disaster. - Chiyo was corrupted during the conflict. - other Inazuman
losses should be represented as separate child events with their own
sources.

The Sacred Sakura is a particularly important **time-anomaly** event and
should not be forced into ordinary linear causality without notes.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 21. Cataclysm --- Mondstadt lane

**Date:** \~500 BP\
**Status:** CONFIRMED.

A major Cataclysm event in Mondstadt was the arrival of **Durin**, a
creation of Rhinedottir.

Dvalin fought Durin, with Barbatos involved in the defense of Mondstadt.
Durin was defeated over Dragonspine.

Durin's corrupted blood later became part of Dragonspine's ongoing
environmental/lore state and affected Dvalin.

Create separate linked entities: - Durin - Dvalin - Barbatos -
Dragonspine - Rhinedottir - Cataclysm

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 22. Cataclysm --- Fontaine / Sumeru border lane

**Date:** \~500 BP\
**Status:** CONFIRMED.

The previous Hydro Archon **Egeria** died during the Cataclysm in the
region now associated with Tunigi Hollow / Vourukasha Oasis.

Her remains became the **Amrita**.

Greater Lord Rukkhadevata used this state to grow the **Harvisptokhm**,
anchoring Egeria's consciousness and helping seal/contain Abyssal
defilement.

The divine bird **Simurgh**, created from Khvarena associated with Nabu
Malikata's preparations for the future disaster, eventually sacrificed
herself and dispersed into Khvarena/Pari-related forms to resist the
corruption.

This event crosses region categories and should not be forced into only
"Fontaine" or only "Sumeru."

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 23. Cataclysm --- Natlan lane

**Date:** \~500 BP\
**Status:** CONFIRMED broad event.

The Abyss invaded Natlan and threatened the Night Kingdom, whose system
had already been created because the region's original Ley Lines were
damaged in the ancient War of Funerary Flame.

This creates a useful timeline connection:

Ancient War of Funerary Flame → destruction of Natlan's original Ley

seat. - Focalors succeeds Egeria. - Fatui develop under the

the Cataclysm remain active.

**Date:** present\
- Traveler awakens and travels the nations. - Irminsul rewrite occurs in
Sumeru. - Fontaine prophecy resolves. - Hydro Divine Throne destroyed;
Authority returns to Neuvillette. - Natlan crisis reveals more of
ancient dragon/Abyss history. - Nod-Krai / lunar storyline reveals
additional information about the Three Moons, Descenders, Saarelainen,

# 36. Final editorial rules

1.  **Never use a community wiki as the final citation for an important
    lore claim if the primary in-game source is identifiable.**
2.  Community wikis are useful as indexes and cross-reference tools.
3.  Preserve the name of the actual quest/book/item/dialogue in
    `Source`.
4.  Do not invent an official URL when no stable official transcript
    exists.
5.  Separate narrator belief from objective fact.
6.  Separate old in-universe myths from later revealed history.
7.  Preserve contradictions.
8.  Preserve uncertainty.
9.  Never assign an exact BP date solely to make the timeline visually
    convenient.
10. Never draw a precise historical border solely because the map looks
    empty.
11. Keep `Gnosis`, `Divine Throne`, and `Elemental Authority` separate.
12. Keep `Abyss`, `Abyss Order`, `Forbidden Knowledge`, and `Khaenri'ah`
    separate.
13. Keep `Celestia`, `Heavenly Principles`, `Primordial One`, and
    `Sustainer` separate unless a claim explicitly establishes identity.
14. Treat Irminsul-modified history as a special data problem: retain
    both underlying event knowledge and current in-world memory where
    known.
15. Add `introducedInVersion` aggressively for recent lunar/Nod-Krai
    lore because it is likely to receive further clarification in
    subsequent patches.
